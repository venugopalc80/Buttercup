The Buttercup story

From a home kitchen to its own front door.

Buttercup started as a home-based passion project in July 2025. Since then it has served around 50,000 puddings, sold out pop-ups across Manchester and built a community of more than 10,000 followers.

The next chapter is a standalone pudding bar, with new flavours, refreshed toppings, drinks in development and rotating specials.
