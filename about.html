Our story

Small cups.
Big energy.

Buttercup is a Manchester pudding brand built around one simple idea: take a nostalgic dessert, make it ridiculously good, and give people a reason to come back for another cup.

From pop-up energy to a home of our own.

Buttercup has grown through pop-ups, residencies and a community that follows every flavour drop. The next chapter is a dedicated Manchester city-centre home.

The brand keeps the menu focused: creamy puddings, nostalgic textures, fresh ingredients and enough personality to make a simple dessert feel like an occasion.
